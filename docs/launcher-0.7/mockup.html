Help

Home
The big line tells you if everything works: League open, helper running, site reachable. "I'm in" checks you in for game night; who else is playing stays on the site.
Clips
Instant replay keeps the last 30 seconds while League runs. Press Ctrl+Alt+S and it's saved, named after your champion and score, and small enough for Discord. Off by default; it uses about 1–3 % of your graphics card while on.
Performance
CPU, GPU, memory and VRAM, and which apps use the most. It only measures while the page is open, so it costs nothing in the background.
Network
Ping and packet loss to the EUW server. "Where the time goes" shows if lag comes from your Wi-Fi, your provider or Riot.
Game mode
Before game night it checks for a pending restart and League patches. When a game starts it switches the power plan and lowers your music, and puts both back afterwards.
Audio
Ctrl+Alt+M mutes your microphone in Windows itself, so Discord, League and your browser all go quiet. Ctrl+Alt+H switches between headset, speakers and monitor.
Fair play
The helper only reads the League client on this PC. It never clicks, picks or changes anything, and only custom games go to Bridge Night.
